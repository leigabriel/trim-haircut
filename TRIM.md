# Trim: A Hairstyle Discovery and Recommendation Application

- Finding the right haircut shouldn't be guesswork.
- Many people choose a hairstyle from trends, photos of other faces, or a barber's quick suggestion, and end up with a cut that doesn't suit them.
- Trim is a web and mobile application. It analyzes a customer's face shape, recommends hairstyles that suit it, and lets the customer book an appointment with the shop's barbers.

## Features:

### Face shape analysis:
- Scans the user's face through the camera.
- Detects the face shape (oval, round, square, heart, or oblong).

### Hairstyle recommendations:
- Styles matched to face shape, hair type, and length.
- Searchable gallery with filters, favorites, and recommendation history.

### Appointment booking:
- Choose a barber, service, date, and time slot.
- Attach the recommended hairstyle as a reference for the barber.
- Booking status, reschedule or cancel, and reminders.
- Ratings and feedback after the haircut.

### Shop information:
- Services and prices, barber profiles, portfolio photos, opening hours, and announcements.

### Admin and barber dashboard (web):
- Manage bookings, barber schedules, services, and prices.
- Manage the hairstyle catalog and face shape rules.
- Manage customer accounts.
- Reports on popular hairstyles, face shapes, and bookings.

### AI hair advisor:
- Chatbot for hairstyle and hair care questions.
